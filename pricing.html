03 / FAQs

Before you get started.

Answers to a few common questions about trial access, pricing and how Brikito works.

What happens after the free trial?

Your Standard plan continues at ₹12,999 per month. Cancel any time before day 14 and you won’t be charged.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Upgrade to Professional any time; your data and groups stay as they are.

Do prices include GST?

No. GST is charged extra as applicable.

Does AI create anything without us?

No. AI drafts; a person always confirms.

Do you work in regional languages?

Yes. Speak or type in Tamil, Hindi, English and other Indian languages.
