03 / FAQs

Before you get started.

Still figuring out what your team needs? These are good questions to start with.

How much does Brikito cost?

We scope pricing around your project count, team size, modules, and rollout requirements. Book a demo for a quote based on your setup.

Can we start with one site?

Yes. Tell us about your first site and what you want to organise. We can discuss a starting scope that can evolve with your needs.

Which features are included?

Brikito brings together communication, attendance, documents, dashboards, meetings and mail, with Brik AI built into the workspace. We’ll confirm the right scope for your team.

Can we use Brikito across multiple projects?

Brikito is designed for teams that need site-level coordination and office-level visibility across projects. Discuss your portfolio during a demo.

Is there a self-serve checkout?

This page does not offer online checkout or fixed public prices. The next step is a conversation so the setup matches your workflows.

How do we get a quote?

Use the Book a demo page and share your project context. The team can talk through fit, rollout, and pricing with you.
