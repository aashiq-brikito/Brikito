1. What Brik AI does

Brikito includes AI features called Brik AI. They read the content you add to your projects (site photos, videos, drawings, specifications, documents, reports and messages) and return text results: quality and progress findings, summaries, answers to questions, and suggested entries for forms. Brik AI does not create images, designs, logos, music or videos, and it does not produce pictures or likenesses of people.

2. The AI models we use

Brik AI combines two kinds of model:

  • Brik AI (Brik 0.0.1): Brikito’s own AI model, built and operated by Brikito Private Limited.
  • Enterprise foundation models: used alongside Brik AI and accessed only through enterprise cloud platforms, namely Amazon Bedrock and Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service. We never use the free or consumer versions of any AI service.

Details of the models and the regions where your data is processed are available to customers under a non-disclosure agreement on request.

3. Your data is never used to train AI

We do not use your inputs (prompts, files, photos, drawings and other project data) or the outputs Brik AI produces to train or improve any AI model, including Brikito’s own model. Our enterprise AI providers are also contractually barred from training their models on your data. If we ever wish to use your data to improve Brik AI, we will ask for your written consent first, and you may refuse or withdraw it at any time.

4. You own your data and the outputs

All inputs and outputs belong exclusively to you. Neither Brikito nor our AI providers claim ownership of, or any licence to use, your data or outputs for any purpose other than providing the service to you.

5. How your data is handled

Your data is sent to an AI model only when an AI feature is used. It is encrypted in transit and at rest, and our AI providers may process it only to deliver the service. Where a provider keeps data briefly for abuse monitoring, we turn this off or ask for it to be turned off wherever the provider allows.

6. People in photos

Brik AI does not identify, recognise or describe individuals. Where a person appears in a site photo, it reports only site safety observations, such as “worker without a safety harness”, and never a name, identity or facial details.

7. Intellectual property

Brik AI analyses your own material and returns findings about it, so it does not generate content that reproduces other people’s copyrighted works, trademarks or likenesses. Our enterprise AI providers give contractual protection against copyright claims arising from their models’ outputs, and we keep their content filters, including protected-material detection, switched on.

8. Safe and appropriate content

Our enterprise AI providers publish their efforts to exclude harmful material, such as child sexual abuse material, hate speech and discriminatory content, from their training data and outputs.

Brik AI is developed and extensively trained using multiple construction-industry datasets, domain-specific knowledge, and insights from construction experts to ensure its capabilities are tailored to real-world construction workflows and project requirements.

Brik AI is built from screened data, and we apply our own controls so that Brik AI is used only for construction project work.

9. Human review

Brik AI suggests; people decide. AI findings are reviewed and approved by an authorised person, such as a QC engineer or project manager, before they are acted on. No record is created or approved by AI alone.

10. Turning AI off

An organisation’s administrator can switch off Brik AI features for the whole organisation or for individual projects. When AI is switched off, no project data is sent to any AI model.

11. Our responsibility

Brikito Private Limited is responsible to its customers for the AI features in its service, including Brikito’s own model, the third-party AI models it integrates, and their outputs. This responsibility is set out in our Terms of Service and the customer agreement.
