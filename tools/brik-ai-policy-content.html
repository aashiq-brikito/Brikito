1. What Brik AI does

Brikito includes AI features called Brik AI. They read the content you add to your projects, such as site photos, videos, drawings, documents, reports and messages, and return text results: quality and progress findings, summaries, answers to questions, and suggested entries for forms. Brik AI does not create images, designs, logos, music or videos, and it does not produce pictures or likenesses of people.

2. The AI models we use

Brik AI runs on large language and vision models from established providers, accessed through enterprise cloud platforms such as Amazon Bedrock and Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service. We use only the providers’ commercial (business) services, never their free or consumer versions. On request, we will tell an organisation which models and platforms process its data and in which region.

3. Your data is not used to train AI

We do not use your inputs (prompts, files, photos, drawings and other project data) or the AI outputs to train or improve any AI model. Under our agreements with them, our AI providers may not train their models on your data either. If we ever want to use your data to improve Brik AI, we will ask for your written permission first, and you can refuse or withdraw it at any time.

4. You own your data and the outputs

Your inputs and the outputs Brik AI produces from them belong to you. Neither Brikito nor our AI providers claim any ownership of them, any licence over them, or any right to use them for other purposes. We process them only to provide the service to you.

5. How data is handled

Your data is sent to an AI model only when you use an AI feature. It is encrypted in transit and at rest. Our AI providers handle it under contracts that limit its use to delivering the service. Some providers keep data for a short period to monitor abuse; where possible, we turn this off or ask for it to be turned off.

6. People in photos

Brik AI does not identify, recognise or describe individuals. Where a person appears in a site photo, it reports only site safety observations, for example “worker without a safety harness”, and never a name, identity or facial details.

7. Intellectual property

Brik AI analyses your own material and returns findings about it, so it does not generate content that copies other people’s copyrighted works, trademarks or likenesses. Our AI providers also give contractual protection against copyright claims arising from their models’ outputs, and we keep the content filters they provide switched on.

8. Safe and appropriate content

We use AI providers that publish their efforts to exclude harmful material, such as child sexual abuse material, hate speech and discriminatory content, from their training data and outputs. We also apply our own controls so that Brik AI is used only for construction project work.

9. Human review

Brik AI suggests; people decide. Quality findings are reviewed by an authorised person, such as a QC engineer or project manager, before they are sent to the site team, and no record is created or approved by AI alone.

10. Turning AI off

An organisation’s administrator can switch off Brik AI features for the whole organisation or for individual projects. When AI is off, no project data is sent to any AI model.

11. Our responsibility

Brikito is responsible to its customers for the AI features in its service, including the AI models it chooses to use and their outputs, as set out in our Terms of Service and any customer agreement.
