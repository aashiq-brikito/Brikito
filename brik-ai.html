Brik AI, answered.

What is an AI assistant for construction?
Software that reads what your project produces, such as chats, voice notes, drawings and records, answers plain-language questions about it, and drafts replies, POs and reports.
How is Brik AI different from ChatGPT?
Brik answers from your live Brikito project data (chats, attendance, stock, drawings and approvals), not general knowledge, and every answer links to its source.
Does it send messages or place orders on its own?
No. A person always confirms before anything is sent, ordered or published.
Which languages does it understand?
English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Bengali, typed or spoken.
Can it show data people shouldn’t see?
No. Answers are scoped by role and by project.
Which plans include Brik AI?
Brik AI is in every plan. Standard includes the core assistant; Professional and Enterprise include the full assistant. See pricing
