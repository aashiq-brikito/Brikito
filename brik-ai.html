Questions

Brik AI, answered.

What is an AI assistant for construction?

An AI assistant for construction is software that reads what a project team already produces, such as site messages, voice notes, drawings, attendance and purchase records, and lets anyone ask questions about the project in plain language. Brik AI also drafts replies, purchase orders and reports so the office spends less time retyping what the site has already said.

How is Brik AI different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT answers from general knowledge. Brik AI answers from your live project data inside Brikito: your chats, attendance, stock, drawings and approvals. It knows which project and role you are asking from, and it links its answers back to the messages and records they came from.

Does Brik AI send messages or place orders on its own?

No. Brik AI drafts; a person always confirms. Replies, purchase orders and reports are prepared for the right person to review and approve before anything is sent.

Which languages does Brik AI understand?

Your team can type or send voice notes in English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Bengali. Brik transcribes and structures them so the office reads one clean version.

Can Brik AI show people data they should not see?

No. Every answer is scoped to the person's role and projects. A contractor sees what a contractor is allowed to see; a project manager sees their projects; leadership sees the portfolio.

Which Brikito plans include Brik AI?

Brik AI is built into every Brikito plan. Standard includes the core assistant, and Professional and Enterprise add the full assistant that answers detailed questions such as cement used, delays and attendance across your projects. See pricing for details.
