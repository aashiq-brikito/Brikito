Our story

Construction runs on chat. Its software never did.

On most sites, the real work happens across WhatsApp groups, paper registers, drawing folders, email and spreadsheets. Cost leaks between them, and people lose hours chasing what was already said.

Construction software was supposed to fix that. But the single largest reason it fails is simple: the people on site won't use it. Forms and portals lose to a voice note every time.

So we started from the other end. Brikito is as simple as the chat your crew already uses, in their own language, and Brik AI does the structuring in the background, so the office gets the record without the site doing extra work.
