Our story

Construction runs on chat. Its software never did.

Site work is spread across WhatsApp groups, paper registers, drawing folders, email and spreadsheets. Most construction software fails for one reason: the site never adopts it. Brikito is simple enough for the crew, and Brik AI structures everything for the office.

Our mission

Make construction simple to run, for every site team.

Our vision

The AI operating system for real estate development.

What we stand for

Four non-negotiables, written before the code.

01 · Authority

Your PM is the checker. Always.

Brik drafts. Nothing is sent, ordered or published until a person approves it.

02 · Grounding

Answers from your project. Nothing else.

Every figure comes from live project data, linked to its source.

03 · Adoption

Built for the site, not the office.

Made for site crews first, in the languages they speak.

04 · Privacy

Presence, not surveillance.

Selfie check-in confirms presence only. Role-based access per project. Data hosted in India and exportable on demand.
