About Brikito CONSTRUCTION MADE SIMPLE

We're making construction simple to run.

Brikito is building the AI operating system for real estate development. It starts with one app the whole site actually uses, with Brik AI inside, so every message, photo and voice note becomes a record, an approval, a report and a live view of the project.

Talk to our team ↗ Our mission
Why we exist

Construction runs on chat. Its software never did.

On most sites the real work happens across WhatsApp groups, paper registers, drawing folders, email and spreadsheets. Cost leaks between them, and people lose hours chasing what was already said.

The problem

Six tools, no single record.

Updates, attendance, indents and drawings live in different places, so nobody sees the whole project at once.

The real reason

The site won't touch office software.

The single largest reason construction software fails is that the people on the ground don't use it. Forms and portals lose to a voice note every time.

Our answer

Meet the site where it already works.

Brikito is as simple as the chat your crew already uses, in their own language. Brik AI does the structuring so nobody has to learn a new way of working.

What we're building

One app for the whole site. Brik AI at the centre.

Each product does one job well, and Brik AI connects them, from foundation to handover.

Our commitments

Four non-negotiables, written before the code.

i. Authority

Your PM is the checker. Always.

Nothing is auto-sent. Every reply, indent and order is drafted by Brik and held until your project manager approves it.

ii. Grounding

Answers from your project. Nothing else.

Every figure Brik gives comes from your live project data, not a generic template or an invented number.

iii. Adoption

Built for the site, not the office.

One app, in the languages the crew speaks, as simple as the chat they already use.

iv. Privacy

Presence, not surveillance.

Each person sees only what their role permits, scoped per project. Data is hosted in India and exportable on demand.
