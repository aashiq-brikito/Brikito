One console, every role

Set up for how you work. On every project.

Brik asks a few simple questions when you create a project. The Console then shows what your role on that project needs.

Builder

You do the work with your own team and subcontractors. Labour, store, DPRs and bills come first.

General contractor

You manage package contractors and report to the client. Submittals, transmittals and vendor scores come first.

Client

You own the project. Progress, quality and payments, without chasing anyone.

Web and mobile

The office on the Console. The site on the app.

Managers run the project from the Console on a laptop. Site teams join by phone number and work from the mobile app. Both see the same records, live.
