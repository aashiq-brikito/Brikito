Questions

BrikChat, answered.

Is BrikChat a WhatsApp alternative for construction teams?

Yes. BrikChat feels as familiar as WhatsApp, with groups, DMs, voice notes and photos, but it belongs to the project instead of personal phones. Brik AI reads every message and turns it into structured work, so decisions, quantities and approvals do not get buried in a group thread.

Why is WhatsApp not enough for construction site communication?

WhatsApp was built for personal messaging. On a construction project it scatters decisions across dozens of groups, makes voice notes impossible to search, leaves no structured record of indents or attendance, and keeps project history on personal phones that leave when people do.

Does my site team need training to use BrikChat?

Very little. If your team can send a WhatsApp message or voice note, they can use BrikChat. They keep talking the way they do today; Brik AI does the structuring in the background.

Can site teams send voice notes in regional languages?

Yes. BrikChat understands English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Bengali. Voice notes are transcribed and tagged so the office can read and search them.

Does Brik AI reply or place orders in the chat automatically?

No. Brik drafts replies, indents and orders, and the right person approves them before anything is sent.

BrikChat is in active development and is the first product on the Brikito roadmap. Book a demo to see it on one of your live projects and join early access.
