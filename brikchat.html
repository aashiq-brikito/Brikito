How it works

From voice note to approved indent. Before lunch.

01

Someone posts

A contractor sends a Tamil voice note: 50 bags of OPC 53 for Block B, tomorrow.

02

Brik reads it

Transcribes it, tags it as a ticket, and pulls out quantity, grade and location.

03

Brik structures it

Raises the indent, checks stock, and drafts a PO to the usual vendor.

04

The PM approves

Priya approves in one tap from the source message. The site gets confirmation in chat.

BrikChat, answered.

Is BrikChat a WhatsApp alternative for construction teams?
Yes. It feels familiar, but the chat belongs to the project, and Brik turns messages into structured work.
Why isn’t WhatsApp enough?
It scatters decisions, voice notes can’t be searched, nothing becomes a record, and history stays on personal phones.
Does the site team need training?
Very little. If they can send a WhatsApp message or a voice note, they can use BrikChat.
Can teams send voice notes in regional languages?
Yes, in all seven supported languages, with transcription and tagging.
Does Brik reply or order automatically?
No. Brik drafts replies, indents and orders. A person approves before anything is sent.
Is it available now?
BrikChat is in active development and is first on the roadmap. Book a demo to join early access.
