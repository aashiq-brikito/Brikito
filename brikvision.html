Questions

BrikVision, answered.

What is AI CCTV monitoring for construction sites?

AI CCTV monitoring uses computer vision on a site's camera feeds to understand what is happening, not just record it. Instead of someone watching hours of footage, the software counts people, detects deliveries, checks for helmets and safety vests, and tracks visible progress, then reports it as data and alerts.

Do I need to buy new cameras for BrikVision?

BrikVision is designed to work with the CCTV cameras already installed on your site. During the pilot our team checks your camera setup and placement and recommends changes only where coverage is missing.

What can BrikVision detect on a construction site?

BrikVision tracks labour presence and headcount, material deliveries and movement, PPE such as helmets and safety vests, activity in work zones, and visible progress. Site photos can also be checked for quality and PPE, and delivery notes read and matched against receipts.

How does AI CCTV improve construction site safety?

It watches every camera continuously and flags safety gaps, such as workers without helmets or people in restricted zones, as they happen. Supervisors get an alert with the camera, time and frame, so they can act on site the same hour instead of discovering it later.

Does a person review BrikVision alerts?

Yes. BrikVision flags and counts; a person decides. Alerts go to the right supervisor with the evidence attached, and nothing is reported against a worker or contractor without someone reviewing it.

Is BrikVision available now?

BrikVision is on the Brikito roadmap and is part of the Enterprise plan, with pilots available on request. Book a demo to discuss a pilot on one of your sites.
