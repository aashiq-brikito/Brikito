BrikVision, answered.

What is AI CCTV monitoring for construction sites?
Software that analyses your site camera feeds to count people, log deliveries, check PPE and track visible progress, then sends numbers and alerts instead of footage.
Do I need new cameras?
Usually not. Our team connects your existing cameras and checks coverage of the gate, work zones and stores.
What can it detect?
People and headcount by zone, gate entries and deliveries, missing helmets and vests, people in restricted zones, and active work zones.
How does it improve site safety?
PPE and zone alerts reach the safety lead while the gap is happening, with the frame attached.
Does a person review alerts?
Yes. Nothing is reported against a worker or contractor until a person has reviewed the evidence.
Is BrikVision available now?
BrikVision is on the roadmap and part of the Enterprise plan. Pilots are available on request. Request a pilot
