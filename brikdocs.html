BrikDocs, answered.

What is a DPR?
A Daily Progress Report records the day’s work, manpower, materials, issues and next steps. BrikDocs builds it from what the team logged.
What is a BBS?
A Bar Bending Schedule lists every reinforcement bar: mark, shape, diameter, cut length and weight, taken from the structural drawings.
Which reports can BrikDocs generate?
DPR, DLR, BOQ, BBS, estimation, schedule, fabrication drawings, GRN and stock statements, plus indents, POs, measurement books, inspections, snag lists and expenses.
Where does each report’s data come from?
From records already in Brikito: chat updates, attendance, indents, GRNs, drawings and plans. Each figure links to its source.
How are drawing revisions handled?
Every upload is versioned. The latest revision is tagged, older ones are marked superseded.
Is BrikDocs available now?
Standard DPR and DLR PDFs are in every plan. Comprehensive and client-ready reports are in Professional. The full suite, with AI document search and drawing revision control, is in Enterprise pilot. See pricing
