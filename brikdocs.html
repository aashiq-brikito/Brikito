Questions

BrikDocs, answered.

What is a DPR in construction?

A DPR, or Daily Progress Report, records what happened on a construction site in a day: work completed against plan, manpower, materials received and used, equipment, issues and photos. BrikDocs builds the DPR automatically from the updates, attendance and material entries your team already posted.

What is a BBS in construction?

A BBS, or Bar Bending Schedule, lists every reinforcement bar in a structural member with its shape, diameter, cut length, number and weight. It is used to cut and bend steel correctly and to estimate steel quantities. BrikDocs is designed to generate the BBS alongside fabrication drawings.

Which construction reports can BrikDocs generate?

BrikDocs covers DPR, DLR, BOQ, BBS, estimation, schedules, fabrication drawings, GRN and stock statements, along with client-ready PDFs for indents, purchase orders, measurement books, inspections, snags and expenses.

Where does the data for each report come from?

From the work already logged in Brikito: progress updates and voice notes in BrikChat, selfie attendance, indents, POs and GRNs, and the drawings in your document library. Nobody has to fill a separate form at the end of the day.

How does BrikDocs handle drawing revisions?

Every drawing, structural plan, BOQ, agreement and certificate is version controlled. The latest revision is always tagged, so the site works from the current drawing and older revisions stay available for reference.

Is BrikDocs available now?

Standard DPR and DLR PDF reports are part of every Brikito plan today, with comprehensive reports and client-ready PDFs in Professional. The full BrikDocs suite, including AI document search and drawing revision control, is next on the roadmap and available to Enterprise customers as a pilot. See pricing.
